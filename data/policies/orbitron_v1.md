## 1 Scope and Contacts

These guidelines apply to all Orbitron Finance employees using company transport. Transport is operated by Nimbus Rides. For anything not covered here, call the transport desk on helpline ext. 7720.

## 2 Eligibility

Employees on rotational shifts or night shifts are eligible for company transport. A night shift is any shift that ends after 9:00 PM or starts before 6:00 AM. Employees on fixed general shifts are not eligible.

## 3 Booking Cut-off

Every trip must be booked at least 2 hours before the scheduled pickup time. Requests made less than 2 hours before the scheduled pickup time are not accepted.

## 4 Night Shift Safety

All employees are provided a security escort on drops departing after 11:00 PM. Drops departing at or before 11:00 PM do not include a security escort.

## 5 Weekend Pickups

Pickups and drops on Saturdays and Sundays are available with approval from the Transport Head.

## 6 Holiday Pickups

Pickups and drops on company holidays are available if requested at least 24 hours before the scheduled pickup time.

## 7 Ad-hoc Trips

Ad-hoc trips are trips to client sites and late-evening trips outside the roster; no other trips count as ad-hoc trips. Ad-hoc trips must be approved by the employee's reporting manager. Ad-hoc trip spending is capped at ₹2,500 per employee per calendar month.

## 8 Guest Travel

Only interview candidates may travel as guests in company cabs, and only when the trip is arranged by the HR recruiting team.

## 9 No-show Policy

A no-show is recorded when an employee does not board within 5 minutes of the cab's arrival. 1 or 2 no-shows in a calendar month result in a reminder. 3 or more no-shows in a calendar month result in a 30-day suspension of company transport.

## 10 Pickup Changes

Employees may change their pickup address after the booking cut-off once per calendar week, through the transport desk.
