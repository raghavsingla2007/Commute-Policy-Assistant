## 1 Scope and Contacts

This policy applies to all Kestrel Systems employees who use company-arranged transport between home and office. Cabs are operated by our vendor partner Falcon Fleet. For any question this policy does not answer, contact the Kestrel transport desk on helpline ext. 4411.

## 2 Eligibility

Employees in Grade L1 to Grade L4 (inclusive) are eligible for scheduled home pickups and drops. Employees in Grade L5 and above are not eligible for scheduled transport.

## 3 Booking Cut-off

Every pickup or drop must be booked at least 4 hours before the scheduled pickup time. Requests made less than 4 hours before the scheduled pickup time are not accepted.

## 4 Night Shift Safety

Women employees are provided a security escort on drops departing after 10:00 PM. Drops departing at or before 10:00 PM do not include a security escort. The escort is a trained guard supplied by Falcon Fleet who stays in the cab until the employee reaches home.

## 5 Weekend Pickups

Pickups and drops on Saturdays are available only with prior approval from the employee's reporting manager. No company transport is provided on Sundays.

## 6 Ad-hoc Trips

An ad-hoc trip is any trip outside the employee's regular roster. Ad-hoc trips must be approved by the employee's reporting manager before the trip. The cost cap for an ad-hoc trip is ₹800 per trip; any amount above the cap is paid by the employee.

## 7 Airport Trips

Airport trips for official travel must be approved by the employee's reporting manager at least 24 hours before the pickup time.

## 8 Guest Travel

Guests, including family members and visitors, are not allowed to travel in Kestrel company cabs.

## 9 No-show Policy

A no-show is recorded when an employee does not board within 5 minutes of the cab's arrival at the pickup point. 2 no-shows in a calendar month result in a written warning. 3 or more no-shows in a calendar month result in a 7-day suspension of company transport.

## 10 Changes and Cancellations

Pickup address changes are not allowed after the booking cut-off. A trip cancelled less than 2 hours before the scheduled pickup time incurs a late cancellation fee of ₹100.
